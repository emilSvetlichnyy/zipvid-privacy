Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20.11.2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the application "Zipvid: Video Compressor" (the "App") handles your information.

1. General Information

The App compresses videos from your photo library entirely on your device. Your videos are never uploaded to our servers. The App has no accounts and shows no ads. The App uses Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage information to help improve the App.

2. Personal Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Contacts
  • Videos, photos or other media files
  • File names, dates or locations of your media
  • Precise device location

3. Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your photo library to list your videos, compress them and save the compressed copies. If you choose, the App can delete the original videos; iOS asks for your confirmation every time. All processing takes place on your device.

If a video is stored in iCloud Photos, iOS downloads it from your iCloud account to your device before compression. This is done by Apple, not by us.

4. Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to collect anonymous usage information. It may include:

  • App launches and screens viewed
  • Feature usage, for example whether a compression finished, was cancelled or failed
  • Approximate sizes and counts, grouped into ranges (never exact values or file names)
  • Purchase-related events
  • Device model, operating system version and country or region
  • An anonymous Firebase App Instance ID

This information is used only to understand how the App is used and to improve it. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or for tracking you across other companies' apps and websites.

5. Local Data Storage

App settings, the free-compression counter and your Pro status are stored locally on your device. Deleting the App removes this data.

6. Purchases

The App offers a one-time Zipvid Pro purchase. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store — payment processing.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — anonymous analytics, processed under Google's privacy policy.

8. Internet Access

Internet access is used only to verify purchases with the App Store, to download videos from iCloud (by iOS) and to send anonymous analytics events. Your videos are never uploaded.

9. Your Rights

Because we do not store your personal information, we cannot access, modify or delete it on your behalf. You can remove all locally stored data by uninstalling the App.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page together with the updated date.

11. Support and Contact

Questions, bug reports or purchase issues? Write to us and we will reply as soon as possible.
